# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and constructive community.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give feedback constructively.
- Respect privacy, consent, and religious/community sensitivities.
- Focus on the work and its impact.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct harms contributors or the community. For concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.
